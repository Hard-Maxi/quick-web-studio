Client review

“I loved everything: the speed, the simplicity, the understanding”

The founder of Academia Holística SHANI got a landing page for retreats in four countries — and decided to trust the studio with the academy’s whole website.

Quick Web Studio newsroom · Buenos Aires

Ksenia came to us with a brief: a landing page for SHANI, a women’s retreat held in Panama, Colombia, Argentina and Spain, built for Instagram ads.

“I loved everything — the speed, the simplicity and how well they understood me,” she wrote after launch. “No bombarding me with endless questions.”

The site has video above the fold, dates and prices by city, WhatsApp booking with a ready-made message, and markup that lets Google understand the retreat dates.

The result: a new order. “So I’m happy to hand you everything technical and web-related,” says Ksenia. “I plan to rebuild the academy’s main site and add small landing pages for projects.”
